\section{Cross-Coin Evidence}
\label{sec:placebo}

Reserve exposure orders the three coins ex ante: USDC held direct SVB exposure, DAI was
pinned to USDC through the peg-stability module, and USDT held none. Global-games run
models make that exposure the state that sets a coin's run
threshold~\cite{carlssonvandamme1993,morrisshin1998,goldsteinpauzner2005,mzz2025}, giving
the ex-ante fragility ordering USDC, DAI, USDT. Troughs are \$0.877, \$0.886, and
\$0.992 in that order. The deviations from par are $1233$, $1141$, and $76$~bps. The
prices are shown to three figures and the deviations are computed on the unrounded four-figure prints, so the two reconcile at full precision rather than at the precision
shown. DAI's mechanical link to USDC leaves one free
comparison, so the realized ordering has probability $1/2$ and illustrates the ex-ante
ordering without testing it. USDC and DAI clear the $1\%$
break threshold, a deviation of at least one cent from par, by factors of
$12$ and $11$. USDT does not clear it, so the placebo criterion is satisfied: the
exposed coins break and the unexposed coin does not.

\paragraph{USDT as the identifying placebo.} USDT was bid to a premium while USDC and
DAI fell. That pattern is consistent with the crypto safe-haven behavior documented for
USDT~\cite{Baur_2021}. On Coinbase, \$1.24B of \$1.52B in
window volume traded above par, so the premium held on material volume. The
premium is venue- and time-specific. On Coinbase \texttt{USDT-USD} it peaked
at $+266$~bps at 02:00~UTC on 11~March, 71 minutes \emph{before} Circle's
03:11~UTC disclosure. The composite feed peaks later and higher, at $+270$~bps at
19:00~UTC on 13~March. That peak falls after the joint federal backstop of the previous
evening, so it is post-backstop rather than intra-run. The
Coinbase premium responds to the SVB failure itself, because Circle's
USDC-specific disclosure had not yet been made. In the 02:00~UTC
hour, Kraken USDT/USD printed a $+600$-bps high on two trades totaling 225
USDT, against a volume-weighted average premium of $+95$~bps for that hour. A
common-factor repricing of stablecoin risk would move all three coins down
together, so no such repricing can produce the 02:00~UTC premium, which is a
flight-to-safety signature.
