\title{Short Paper: Non-Fundamental by Impossibility: A Public-Data Bound on Panic
and Friction in the March 2023 USDC De-Peg}
\titlerunning{Short Paper: Non-Fundamental by Impossibility}

\institute{}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We give a reusable, public-data instrument for a disclosed-impairment de-peg.
Under reserve-only fundamentals, par redemption when solvent, and recovery in
$[0,1]$ on the impaired fraction $\phi$, fundamental value cannot fall below
$1-\phi$. In March 2023, SVB's failure trapped about $8\%$ of USDC's reserves
and the coin fell to \$0.877, below the \$0.92 floor. Under those three conditions,
the trough requires unremediated-loss probability $q^{\star}=1.54$, exceeding one.
At the disclosed $\phi$, at least $35\%$ of the de-peg is non-fundamental on
the composite feed and at least $19\%$ on Kraken's close. An ex-ante fragility
ordering by reserve exposure matches the realized troughs. A specificity panel
gives one true positive (USDC, 2023), one regulator-quantified, issuer-contested
episode on which the bound returns no verdict (Tether, 2019), and one
degenerate control, not counted (USDT, Terra episode, May 2022, $\phi=0$).

\end{abstract}
