\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

\looseness=-1
A disclosed impairment implies a worst-case floor. The March 2023 USDC trough lies
below it, so at least $35\%$ of the de-peg was non-fundamental by impossibility, and exactly $100\%$
ex post, an identity. Against a small panel, the instrument returns one true positive, and its
specificity is unmeasured (Section~\ref{sec:sensitivity}). Two policy readings follow (Section~\ref{sec:disc}): limit idiosyncratic bank-deposit exposure, and require
the quantified disclosure the instrument depends on. The instrument
requires no structural model, only a price and a publicly quantified $\phi$.
