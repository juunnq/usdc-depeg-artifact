\title{Non-Fundamental by Impossibility: A Public-Data Bound on Panic and Friction in the March 2023 USDC De-Peg}
\titlerunning{Non-Fundamental by Impossibility}
\author{Anonymous Submission to FC 2027}
\authorrunning{Anonymous Submission}
\institute{}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We give a reusable, public-data instrument for a disclosed-impairment de-peg.
If fundamental value is expected disclosed backing, redemption is at par under solvency,
and recovery on the impaired fraction $\phi$ lies in $[0,1]$,
then fundamental value cannot fall below $1-\phi$, and a Corollary extends the floor to
any monotone-utility valuation. In the March 2023 USDC de-peg, SVB's failure trapped
about $8\%$ of reserves and the coin fell to \$0.877, below the \$0.92 floor.
The trough requires unremediated-loss probability $q^{\star}=1.54$, which
exceeds one. At the disclosed $\phi$, at least $35\%$ of the de-peg is non-fundamental on
the composite series, and $19$--$35\%$ across feed troughs. The trough is unrationalizable
for any impairment below $12.3\%$. Hourly evaluation dates the impossibility to nine
breaching hours on
11~March. Two supporting analyses stay outside it. A global-games ex-ante fragility
ordering matches the realized troughs. A specificity panel gives one true positive (USDC,
2023), one regulator-quantified, issuer-contested episode on which the bound returns no
verdict (Tether, 2019), and one degenerate
control (USDT, Terra episode, May 2022, $\phi=0$).

\keywords{Stablecoins \and Bank runs \and Global games \and De-peg \and Financial stability \and Market microstructure}
\end{abstract}
