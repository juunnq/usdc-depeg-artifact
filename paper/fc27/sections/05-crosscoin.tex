\section{Cross-Coin Evidence}
\label{sec:placebo}

A global-games run model (Appendix~\ref{app:model}) yields an ex-ante fragility ordering
for the March 2023 shock, driven by reserve exposure: USDC (direct SVB exposure) most
fragile, DAI (mechanically pinned to USDC through the PSM) next, and USDT (no SVB
exposure) least. This single episode identifies exposure. It does not identify
concentration, since USDC was less concentrated than USDT on the external data
of~\cite{mzz2025} (Appendix~\ref{app:hhi}) and still had the deepest trough. DAI is
mechanically linked to USDC (Appendix~\ref{app:dai}), so the null leaves one free comparison,
USDT against the USDC--DAI pair. Under that null the realized ordering has probability $1/2$,
and we therefore treat the match as illustrative.

\paragraph{Realized ordering.} Troughs are \$0.877 (USDC), \$0.886 (DAI), and \$0.992
(USDT), matching the ex-ante fragility ordering (Figure~\ref{fig:event}a,
Table~\ref{tab:placebo}). USDC and DAI clear the $1\%$ break threshold, a deviation of at
least one cent from par, by factors of $12$ and $11$. That threshold is one we set, and the calm-baseline false-positive check of Appendix~\ref{app:excl} tests
it. USDT does not clear it, so the placebo's
criterion is satisfied, namely that the exposed coins break and the unexposed coin does
not. The de-peg printed on material volume: in the frozen Binance
\texttt{USDCUSDT} klines, the minimum hourly low (\$0.882, 2023-03-11 14:00
UTC) printed on \$109M of volume in that single hour. The series starts after the trough,
so it corroborates depth on volume (Section~\ref{sec:setting}). The
impossibility result holds at every in-window reading, including \$0.882. DAI's break runs through
its documented USDC exposure (Appendix~\ref{app:dai}).

\paragraph{Redemption concentration.} Address-level redemption concentration is
$\mathrm{HHI}=0.256$. The larger of the two dominant conduits is labeled
Coinbase in two public label sets, \texttt{dawsbot/eth-labels} and
\texttt{brianleect/etherscan-labels}, and carries $40.9$ of the $69.6$ points of volume
share (rounded here and given to four figures in Appendix~\ref{app:hhi}). The second conduit's Coinbase attribution comes
from our own on-chain tracing, with no public label. Excluding both conduits leaves the
index at $0.067$. The no-sybil condition, meaning no entity outside the two dominant conduits
splits redemptions across multiple funding addresses, is untestable from the public label
coverage reported in Appendix~\ref{app:hhi}.
Three distinct quantities are easily conflated here. $40.9\%$ is
the largest single address's share of captured redemption volume, and $69.6\%$ is the two
dominant conduits' combined share. A separate figure for USDC's largest monthly arbitrageur,
reported in~\cite{mzz2025}, uses a different unit and is not a replication
(Appendix~\ref{app:hhi}).

\paragraph{USDT as the identifying placebo.} USDT was bid to a premium while USDC and DAI
fell~\cite{Baur_2021}. \$1.24B of \$1.52B in window volume on Coinbase traded above par,
so the Coinbase premium held on material volume. The premium is
venue- and time-specific. On Coinbase \texttt{USDT-USD}, it peaked at $+266$~bps at
02:00~UTC on 11~March, 71 minutes \emph{before} Circle's 03:11~UTC disclosure. The premium
is a response to the SVB failure itself, because Circle's USDC-specific disclosure
had not yet been made. In the 02:00~UTC hour, Kraken USDT/USD printed a $+600$-bps high on two
trades totaling 225 USDT, against a volume-weighted average premium of $+95$~bps for that
hour. A third peak of $+270$~bps appears in the composite feed at
19:00~UTC on 13~March. It follows the joint Treasury/Fed/FDIC
statement of 22:15~UTC on 12~March~\cite{fedtreasfdic2023pressrelease} by 20.8 hours. This
peak is therefore a post-backstop event and is not the flight-to-safety move the
identification argument uses. The 02:00~UTC premium is a flight-to-safety signature, and
no market-wide repricing of stablecoin risk can produce it, because such a repricing would
move all three coins down together.

\paragraph{Coins excluded from the placebo set.} Three further zero-SVB-exposure coins,
USDP, GUSD, and BUSD, fail the $1\%$ break test's diagnostic requirement because their
pre-SVB price feeds already print below \$0.99, and their zero-exposure flow evidence is
consistent with the placebo result (Appendix~\ref{app:excl}).
